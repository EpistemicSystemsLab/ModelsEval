AI model oversight

Two code-free courses for the people who approve, challenge or buy AI models in regulated firms.

Course one

Evaluating AI models

How to judge an evaluation without running one. The course treats every evaluation as an argument, a claim about a model supported by evidence gathered under stated conditions, and trains the reviewer to test that argument.

Topics run from claims and construct validity through benchmarks, contamination, language-model judges and agent testing to the sign-off memo.

  • FormatTen modules, four interactive labs, assessment and certificate
  • LabsJudge agreement, confidence interval against a threshold, vendor report triage, sign-off memo
  • CodingNone
Open the evaluation course

Course two

Auditing AI models

How to commission, read and challenge an independent audit of an AI model developer: who performed it, on what access, under which law, and what the report can support.

  • FormatEight units with checklists and templates
  • UnitsEvaluation, audit and verification; assurance levels; access; independence; evidence and sampling; audit report; regimes compared; commissioning an auditor
  • CodingNone

Who the courses serve

Model risk officers, compliance leads, internal auditors, procurement teams and board secretaries. Each course can be taken on its own. Course one covers the evidence about a model; course two covers the outside parties who examine the developer.

Statements about laws, standards and companies in the audit course rest on sources listed in its manual, with the date each was read.
